### Bahasa Indonesia (default)

Streamy mengirim suara Anda secara langsung ke server radio internet SHOUTcast/Icecast — dan tetap bersiaran meski layar mati.

Untuk penyiar radio internet, pengelola stasiun, atau Anda yang ingin menyiarkan kajian dan murottal dari ponsel: inilah aplikasi siaran profesional yang muat di saku Anda.

● Kualitas audio pilihan Anda — MP3 dari 16 kbps ramah suara hingga 192 kbps kelas studio. Atur bitrate, laju sampel, serta mono atau stereo untuk tiap profil.

Dibuat seperti konsol siaran sungguhan: lampu tally on-air, tombol hentikan sekali sentuh, dan tampilan yang nyaman dipakai di tengah siaran.

### English

Streamy sends your voice live to your SHOUTcast/Icecast internet radio server — and keeps broadcasting even when the screen turns off.

For internet radio broadcasters, station operators, or anyone airing lectures and recitations from a phone: this is a professional broadcast tool that fits in your pocket.

HIGHLIGHTS

● Real radio servers — Native SHOUTcast v2 source protocol (Ultravox 2.1) and ICE/1.0 for Icecast. Live-tested against DNAS 2.6.1.

● Audio quality your way — MP3 from voice-friendly 16 kbps up to studio-class 192 kbps. Pick bitrate, sample rate, and mono or stereo per profile.

● Keeps broadcasting in the background — A foreground service keeps the mic and connection alive with the screen off, and auto-reconnects with growing backoff after network drops.

● Now-playing titles, no admin login — Type in the reciter, surah, or show name while on air and listeners see it instantly. No server admin password needed.

● See your voice as a waveform — Real-time oscilloscope and VU meter drawn straight from your microphone signal, lit like a broadcast tally light.

● Multiple station profiles — Save host, port, password, and audio settings for each server and switch with one tap.

● Standalone MP3 recorder — Save recitations or shows to your device storage, even without broadcasting.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Create a server profile — enter host, port, and password.
2. Choose your audio quality.
3. Tap Broadcast. Your voice flows to your internet radio server, live.

Built like a real broadcast console: on-air tally light, one-tap stop button, and an interface made for use mid-broadcast.

Streamy is a broadcast tool. Audio goes only to the server you configure — the developer collects no data at all.

Ready to go on air? Download Streamy and broadcast your voice to the world.
